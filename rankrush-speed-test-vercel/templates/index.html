Download and upload are estimates for the route between your browser and Rankrush test server, not a guaranteed measurement of your full ISP connection. Response time is browser-to-server HTTP latency, not ICMP ping. Server capacity, hosting location, routing, Wi-Fi quality, and device load affect results.

Rankrush Speed Test runs three small transfer trials to reduce one-off measurement noise. Vercel limits each transfer request, so results may differ from dedicated services such as Ookla Speedtest, especially on fast connections.
